Engineering Economics (EEFM) & PdM Mathematical Formulations

Rigorous equations governing the condition-based asset replacement decision support model.

1. Capital Recovery Cost with Return (CR)

Annualized cost of initial capital investment P recovering salvage value S_n over n years at interest rate i:

CR(n) = (P - S_n)(A/P, i, n) + i · S_n
where (A/P, i, n) = [i(1 + i)^n] / [(1 + i)^n - 1]

2. Condition-Adjusted Weibull Reliability & Risk Cost

As equipment health HI(t) degrades, the characteristic life η contracts dynamically:

η_cond(HI) = η_0 × [HI(t) / 100]^1.6
F(t | HI) = 1 - exp(-[t / η_cond(HI)]^β)
C_risk(t) = F(t | HI) × C_unplanned_failure_consequence

3. Equivalent Uniform Annual Cost (EUAC)

Total equivalent annual cost combining capital recovery, condition-based O&M, and expected downtime risk:

EUAC(n) = CR(n) + ∑_{t=1}^n [ (O&M(t) + C_risk(t)) / (1 + i)^t ] × (A/P, i, n)

4. Marginal Cost Analysis (Defender vs. Challenger)

The marginal cost of retaining the defender for one additional period t:

MC_t = (S_{t-1} - S_t) + i · S_{t-1} + O&M_adj(t) + C_risk(t)
Decision Rule: Replace Defender when MC_t > EUAC*_Challenger or RUL ≤ Lead_Time
